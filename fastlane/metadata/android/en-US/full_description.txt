Monitor and manage Linux, BSD and Windows servers from one app.

A server is reached over SSH, over the ServerBox Monitor agent's HTTP API, or both.

* Status charts: CPU, memory, disk, network, temperature, GPU, S.M.A.R.T, battery
* SSH terminal with a virtual keyboard, a floating window over the rest of the app, and a shell on the device itself
* Files over SFTP, or over SCP where the host has no SFTP subsystem
* Docker containers, processes, systemd services, snippets and custom commands
* Benchmark disk, network and CPU, with the results stored and comparable
* Your servers drawn on a globe, placed on the device without an address leaving it
* Port forwarding, jump hosts and ProxyCommand
* Biometric auth, push notifications, home widgets and a watchOS app
* 15 languages

Especially thanks to dartssh2 & xterm.dart.
